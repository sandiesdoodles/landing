About Cosmos

Estimated Adult Size: ~32–44 lbs (medium)

Cosmos is our little gentleman with a playful spark and a heart full of connection. He has a warm apricot coat with soft, plush curls and a beautiful white chest patch that gives him that classic doodle charm. His expressive eyes and attentive posture make him look like he’s always listening — and he usually is.

Cosmos is confident without being overwhelming. He’s people-focused, responsive, and naturally engaging. He enjoys interaction and playtime, then happily settles in close for affection. He bonds quickly and thrives in a loving, involved home.

Raised right in our home with daily handling and exposure to normal family life, Cosmos is comfortable, socialized, and ready for his forever family. He has had his wellness exam and age-appropriate vaccinations.

If you’re looking for a sweet, affectionate boy with personality and presence who will likely mature into a solid medium-sized companion, Cosmos is ready to steal your heart.
