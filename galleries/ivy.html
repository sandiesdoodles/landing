About Ivy

Ivy is our gentle, soft-hearted little girl with the sweetest presence in the room. She has a calm, observant personality and tends to watch before she leaps — taking everything in with quiet confidence. Ivy bonds deeply and loves being close to her people. She’s the kind of puppy who settles comfortably in your arms and makes eye contact in that peaceful, reassuring way.

Her coat is a light apricot with soft curls that frame her petite, feminine features beautifully. She has the most adorable little white “sock” toes — one of her signature markings that makes her instantly recognizable. Ivy carries a delicate charm without being timid; she’s affectionate, responsive, and naturally people-oriented.

At 11 weeks old, Ivy is at a wonderful stage for joining her forever family. She’s been raised in our home with daily handling and exposure to normal household sounds and routines. She adjusts gently, enjoys attention, and thrives in calm, loving environments. She would do beautifully with a family looking for a cuddly companion who enjoys closeness and connection.

Ivy has had her wellness exam and age-appropriate vaccinations and has been cared for with consistent monitoring and structure since birth.

If you’re looking for a sweet, affectionate little girl with a calm spirit and loving nature, Ivy is ready to bring warmth and companionship into your home.
