About Jasper

Estimated Adult Size: ~28–40 lbs (medium) Jasper is our handsome apricot boy with soft curls and a steady, confident personality. He has a thoughtful expression and a calm presence that makes him feel grounded and dependable. He enjoys engagement and play but carries himself with balance. Jasper is affectionate without being clingy and playful without being overwhelming — a wonderful fit for families wanting both fun and steadiness. Raised in our home with daily handling and normal household exposure, Jasper is comfortable, socialized, and ready to bond deeply with his forever family. He has had his wellness exam and age-appropriate vaccinations. If you’re looking for a confident, loving boy who will likely mature into a beautiful medium-sized companion, Jasper may be the perfect fit.
