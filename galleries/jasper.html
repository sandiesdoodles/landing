About Jasper

Jasper is our confident little explorer with a thoughtful, expressive face and a naturally curious spirit. He carries himself with a quiet strength — alert, observant, and always interested in what’s happening around him. Jasper isn’t overly flashy; instead, he has that steady, intelligent presence that makes him feel grounded and dependable.

He has a warm apricot/caramel coat with soft curls and a distinct white blaze on the top/front of his head, along with a clean white chest patch and touches of white on his paws. Those markings give him a striking, classic doodle look while keeping his expression sweet and balanced. His dark eyes and teddy-bear features add to his charming, slightly serious “little gentleman” vibe.

At 11 weeks old, Jasper’s personality is clearly forming. He enjoys interaction and engagement, and he’s the type of puppy who will likely bond strongly with his person. He’s playful without being wild and attentive without being clingy — a wonderful balance for families who want both affection and confidence in their companion.

Jasper has been raised in our home with daily handling and exposure to normal household life. He’s comfortable being held, enjoys exploring his environment, and adapts well to routine. He has had his wellness exam and age-appropriate vaccinations and has been carefully monitored from the start.

If you’re looking for a handsome, confident boy with intelligence, warmth, and a strong connection potential, Jasper may be the perfect fit for your family.
