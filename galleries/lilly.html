About Lilly

Estimated Adult Size: ~22–34 lbs (small-to-medium)

Lilly is our soft, creamy-apricot sweetheart with a gentle, cuddly nature. Her coat is light and plush with soft curls that give her that classic teddy-bear look. She has a sweet, expressive face and a tender personality that makes her easy to fall in love with.

Lilly is affectionate and adaptable. She enjoys being held, loved on, and included in family time. She’s playful in a gentle way and especially shines during cuddle time. She adjusts beautifully to handling and routine.

Raised in our home and surrounded by daily family interaction, Lilly is comfortable, social, and ready for her forever home. She has had her wellness exam and age-appropriate vaccinations.

If you’re looking for a sweet, snuggly companion who may stay on the smaller side of medium, Lilly may be the one.
