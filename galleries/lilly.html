About Lilly

Lilly is our sweet, adaptable girl with a gentle nature and a bright, curious expression. She carries a soft confidence — happy to explore, but always ready to come back for affection. Lilly adjusts beautifully to handling and routine, making her a wonderful fit for a family looking for a loving, easygoing companion.

She sits in the light apricot-to-cream range, slightly deeper in tone than Willow, with soft curls that give her a classic doodle look. Lilly has warm, expressive eyes and a tender, feminine face that gives her a naturally kind appearance. She’s the kind of puppy who enjoys playtime but truly shines when she’s being loved on.

At 11 weeks old, Lilly is in a prime developmental stage — confident enough to engage, young enough to mold easily into her forever home’s rhythm. She’s been raised in our home with daily human interaction and exposure to normal household sounds and structure. She responds well to attention and has shown herself to be both sweet and adaptable.

Lilly has had her wellness exam and age-appropriate vaccinations and has been carefully monitored as part of our litter since birth.

If you’re looking for a gentle, affectionate little girl with a balanced personality and a loving heart, Lilly would make a beautiful addition to your family.
