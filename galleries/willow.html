About Willow

Estimated Adult Size: ~30–42 lbs (medium) Willow is our warm apricot girl with soft curls and a tender, expressive face. She has a beautiful white chest patch and bright eyes that show both sweetness and curiosity. She carries a calm confidence — affectionate, thoughtful, and steady. Willow enjoys interaction but has a peaceful presence that makes her especially comforting to be around. She loves being close to her people and adapts smoothly to routine. Raised in our home with consistent handling and everyday family life, Willow is comfortable and socialized. She has had her wellness exam and age-appropriate vaccinations. If you’re looking for a loving, balanced companion who will likely mature into a beautiful medium-sized family dog, Willow would make a wonderful addition.
