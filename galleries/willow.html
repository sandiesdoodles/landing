About Willow

Willow is our soft, sweet girl with a gentle presence that instantly makes you feel calm. She has a quiet confidence about her — not shy, just thoughtful. Willow tends to take in her surroundings with bright, curious eyes before stepping forward, and once she trusts you, she melts right into your arms. She’s affectionate, nurturing in nature, and thrives on steady, loving attention.

Her coat falls in the light cream-to-soft apricot lane, giving her a plush, airy look that highlights her expressive face. She has subtle white toes that add to her delicate charm. Willow carries herself with a peaceful energy — the kind of puppy who is content sitting beside you just as much as she enjoys gentle play.

At 11 weeks old, Willow is in that beautiful stage where her personality is clearly forming. She’s been raised in our home with daily handling and consistent exposure to normal household activity. She adjusts smoothly, enjoys being held, and responds well to calm interaction. She would do especially well in a home looking for a steady, affectionate companion.

Willow has had her wellness exam and age-appropriate vaccinations and has been cared for closely since birth.

If you’re looking for a sweet, loving girl with a calm spirit and a soft presence, Willow may be the perfect addition to your family.
