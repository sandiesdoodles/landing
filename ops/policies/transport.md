# Transport policy — Sandies Doodles

Local pickup in Hobe Sound, Florida is preferred.

Buyer-paid transport may be available for **approved families only**. Transport is separate from the puppy price and depends on location and method.

## Rules (non-negotiable)

- No puppy leaves before **8 weeks** old
- Veterinary clearance required
- Required health paperwork / certificate of veterinary inspection
- Full payment cleared before pickup or transport
- Puppy must be travel-ready

## Options

1. **Local pickup** — Preferred. Free.
2. **Meet-up delivery** — Case by case (see fees below)
3. **Approved ground transporter** — Buyer pays transporter directly
4. **Approved flight nanny** — Buyer arranges and pays after approval

We do not promise “ship anywhere,” include transport in puppy price, or release early.

## Meet-up fees (if Sandies drives)

| Distance | Fee |
|---|---|
| Local pickup | Free |
| Within ~1 hour | $100 |
| 1–2 hours | $200 |
| 3–4 hours | $350–$500 |
| Longer | Buyer uses approved transporter / flight nanny |

## Public wording

> Local pickup preferred. Buyer-paid transport options may be available after approval.
